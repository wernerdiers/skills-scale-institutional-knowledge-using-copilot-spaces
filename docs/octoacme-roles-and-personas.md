# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Managers / Project Leads

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers or Project Leads coordinate day-to-day execution across teams. They turn approved plans into an effective delivery cadence, track progress, manage dependencies, and ensure blockers are addressed promptly.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate delivery milestones, iteration plans, and execution checkpoints
- Track progress, dependencies, risks, and blockers across teams
- Facilitate delivery ceremonies and maintain an actionable delivery board
- Escalate issues that require decisions, resources, or scope changes
- Confirm that delivery outcomes and handoffs are ready for the next phase

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Project Managers to maintain the overall plan, status reporting, risk register, and stakeholder communications
- Work with Product Managers to sequence priorities and surface scope or acceptance-criteria issues
- Work with Developers to remove blockers, coordinate dependencies, and protect focus during execution
- Provide sponsors and stakeholders with timely updates on progress, trade-offs, and escalation needs

---

## Product Owners / Business Sponsors

### Role Summary
Product Owners or Business Sponsors represent the business outcome the project is expected to achieve. They clarify priorities, make or sponsor scope decisions, and ensure that delivered work addresses stakeholder and customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Define desired outcomes, success measures, and business constraints
- Confirm priorities and make timely scope or sequencing decisions
- Validate that proposed solutions address the intended business need
- Resolve business-level trade-offs when objectives or stakeholder needs conflict
- Approve completed outcomes when acceptance criteria and business expectations are met

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to align product strategy, roadmap priorities, and project outcomes
- Work with Project Managers and Delivery Managers to make decisions that affect schedule, scope, or resources
- Clarify acceptance criteria with Developers and review delivered results
- Keep executive sponsors and key stakeholders aligned on outcomes, decisions, and changes in direction

---

## Security and Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Partners advise on security, privacy, regulatory, and policy requirements throughout the project lifecycle. They help the team identify controls early and make risks visible before release decisions are made.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security, privacy, compliance, and policy requirements
- Review designs, implementation plans, and release readiness for relevant controls
- Maintain or contribute to security and compliance risks, decisions, and evidence
- Recommend mitigations and define approval conditions or exceptions
- Escalate unresolved high-impact risks through the agreed governance path

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers on secure design, implementation controls, testing, and remediation
- Work with Product Managers and Product Owners to translate policy requirements into backlog items and acceptance criteria
- Work with Project Managers and Delivery Managers to schedule reviews, track mitigations, and report risk status
- Advise stakeholders and sponsors when residual risk requires an explicit business decision or approval

---

## Platform and Developer Experience Enablement Leads

### Role Summary
Platform and Developer Experience Enablement Leads provide the tools, environments, automation, and operational guidance teams need to deliver reliably. They remove platform-related friction without taking ownership of the product decisions or delivery plan.

### Responsibilities
- Ensure development, test, deployment, and observability environments are ready for planned work
- Provide or coordinate reusable tooling, automation, documentation, and support
- Identify platform dependencies, capacity constraints, and operational risks
- Improve developer workflows, build reliability, and delivery feedback loops
- Coordinate operational readiness and handoff requirements before release

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers to resolve tooling, environment, build, and deployment issues
- Work with Project Managers and Delivery Managers to plan platform dependencies and communicate readiness or risks
- Work with Product Managers and Product Owners to understand upcoming product needs and prioritize enablement work
- Coordinate with Security and Compliance Partners to apply required controls in platforms and delivery pipelines

---

## Stakeholder Liaisons / Customer Advocates

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons or Customer Advocates maintain structured communication with business partners, customers, and other affected groups. They ensure that feedback, expectations, and adoption concerns are visible to the people making product and delivery decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Identify stakeholder groups, communication needs, and feedback channels
- Gather and synthesize customer or stakeholder feedback without bypassing prioritization processes
- Communicate relevant project context, decisions, status, and expected impacts
- Surface changes in stakeholder expectations, adoption risks, or emerging concerns
- Support validation, rollout communication, and feedback after delivery

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers and Product Owners to connect feedback to product outcomes and prioritization
- Work with Project Managers and Delivery Managers to coordinate status updates, decision communications, and escalation of expectation gaps
- Provide Developers with validated user context while allowing Product Managers or Product Owners to own requirements and priorities
- Coordinate with sponsors and other stakeholders to keep communications consistent and actionable

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When a project assigns multiple personas to one person, retain the responsibilities and decision boundaries of each persona and make the combined accountability explicit.
